## Engineering starts with purpose

**QASD** is a software engineering and product design studio. We design and build bespoke digital systems, audit existing software, and restore clarity to systems that have accumulated technical debt.

Our name comes from the Arabic **قَصْد** — intention, purpose, and deliberate direction. It shapes how we work: understand the problem, make thoughtful decisions, and build software that people can use, explain, and maintain.

## What we do

| | Focus | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **BUILD** | Bespoke software | Web platforms, mobile applications, backend services, APIs, and business systems built around real requirements. |
| **DESIGN** | Product & UX | Product direction, user journeys, interfaces, and design systems that make the experience clear before implementation. |
| **AUDIT** | Software & architecture | Assess architecture, code quality, UX, performance, and security to give teams a clear view of their system and its risks. |
| **REPAIR** | Technical remediation | Refactor, restructure, or rebuild the parts of a system that make it difficult to change and maintain. |
| **EVOLVE** | Ongoing engineering | A long-term engineering partnership to improve products as their users and requirements evolve. |

## How we work

1. **Understand.** Clarify the problem, users, goals, and constraints. Review what already exists.
2. **Design.** Define the product experience and system architecture before writing the implementation.
3. **Build & verify.** Deliver in clear stages, with code review and checks for usability, performance, and security.
4. **Deliver & evolve.** Establish clear documentation and ownership, then improve the system as needs change.

Our audits turn findings into practical decisions: evidence, impact, severity, and a prioritized path forward. The assessment should be useful to your team whichever engineering partner you choose next.

## What guides us

- **Purpose.** Every feature and architectural decision should have a reason.
- **Craft.** Product design and engineering deserve the same care.
- **Ownership.** We take responsibility for the systems we deliver.
- **Longevity.** Software should remain understandable after its original authors move on.

### AI is a tool. Intent is ours.

We use AI to support research, prototyping, automation, and implementation where it helps. Product judgment, architecture, review, and engineering responsibility stay with the people building the system.

The standard is the quality of the result: correct, intentional, and maintainable software.
